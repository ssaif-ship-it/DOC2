UPI Lite, prepaid wallets and EMI on UPI are three ways a customer may pay through UPI that you might hear about. For UPI Lite and wallets, there is nothing for you to set up. EMI on UPI is not generally available yet.

### UPI Lite

UPI Lite is a feature inside the customer's UPI app. The customer switches it on in their own app and keeps a small balance there for low value payments. There is no setting, API field or payment mode for UPI Lite on Cashfree, and you do not need to enable or integrate anything.

### Prepaid wallets (PPI)

Customers can pay through UPI using a prepaid wallet (PPI) that is linked to their UPI app. These payments are accepted by default along with your other UPI payments, so you do not need to enable anything.

Cashfree treats wallet payments as a separate payment mode, so their rate can differ from bank account UPI payments. Check your pricing or ask your account manager. If you do not want to accept wallet payments, or RuPay credit card payments, on UPI, ask your account manager to switch them off.

### EMI on UPI

EMI on UPI lets a customer who pays with a RuPay credit card through UPI convert the payment into EMI. It is not generally available on Cashfree yet. If you would like to use it, talk to your account manager.
